Shipping

How fast will my order ship?
Most orders ship within 1–3 business days. Custom orders take 5–7 business days to make before they ship. You'll get a tracking link by email.
How much is shipping?
Shipping is $4 flat in the US, and free on orders $25+ ✎. Mix and match anything to get there.
Do you ship internationally?
Currently US only — email me and I'll see what I can do.
When should I order for the holidays?
To arrive by Christmas, order ready-made pieces by Dec 14 and custom pieces by Dec 5.

Returns & exchanges

Can I return something?
Because pieces are handmade and mostly one-of-one, all sales are final — but if anything arrives damaged or isn't right, email me within 7 days with a photo and I'll repair, remake or refund it.
My charm broke — can you fix it?
Yes! Send me a photo. Most repairs are free within the first 30 days.

Will it fit my phone?

How does a phone charm attach?
The cord loops through a lanyard hole or loop on your case. No hole? A thin tether tab sits between your phone and case, so it works with almost any phone — including MagSafe cases.
Will a wrist keychain fit my wrist?
They're made to slip over most adult wrists. Need it bigger or smaller? Add a note at checkout.

Charm care

How do I keep it looking new?
Wipe with a soft, damp cloth. Keep it out of water, away from harsh chemicals and long sun exposure, and avoid snagging it on sharp edges.
