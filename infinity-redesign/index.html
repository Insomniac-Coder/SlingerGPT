Building it

No re-platforming. It's all Squarespace.

The current site runs on Squarespace 7.1, and almost everything here maps to built-in settings and blocks. A little custom CSS covers the pill buttons and card styling.

Renames, for example

Names that say what the thing is help shoppers, on-site search and Google. All 39 are applied in the prototype.

Worth knowing: character designs

Some pieces use character beads (Hello Kitty, Kuromi, Stitch, Pooh & friends). Lots of small makers sell these, but marketplaces and payment processors do act on trademark complaints. Safer to keep character names out of product titles and lead marketing with her original designs — the mockups do that.
